Reaching for a frontier model is the reasonable default, because it is the thing that works on everything. On one narrow, high-volume task it is the expensive way to be less accurate.

That is the measurement, not the argument.   Everything below is how it was established — including the tier that turned out to be unnecessary, and the case where the model is confidently wrong and says nothing.
